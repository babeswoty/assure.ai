Control: PILOT-C-UAM-003 - Quarterly Privileged Access Review
Interviewee: R. Fischer, Senior Systems Administrator (fictitious person)

[L01] Interviewer: How does the privileged access review work?
[L02] Interviewee: Every quarter, I pull a report of everyone with admin-level access across our core systems.
[L03] Interviewer: What do you actually do with that report?
[L04] Interviewee: I go through it line by line and confirm each person still needs that level of access for their current role, and I sign off on the review.
[L05] Interviewer: Who is "I" in this case - what's your role relative to granting that access in the first place?
[L06] Interviewee: That's me too, actually - I'm also the one who grants admin access whenever someone requests it during the quarter.
[L07] Interviewer: So the same person both grants the access during the quarter and reviews it at quarter-end?
[L08] Interviewee: Correct, it's always been me for both. We're a small IT team, so there isn't really anyone else who understands the systems well enough to review it.
[L09] Interviewer: When exactly does the review happen?
[L10] Interviewee: Last week of every quarter - it's on my calendar as a recurring task.
[L11] Interviewer: Why is the review performed?
[L12] Interviewee: To make sure nobody's still got admin rights from a role they've since moved out of, or a temporary access grant that was never removed.
[L13] Interviewer: Where do you document the review?
[L14] Interviewee: I save the reviewed spreadsheet with my sign-off date in a shared drive folder called "Access Reviews."
[L15] Interviewer: How do you decide whether someone still needs the access?
[L16] Interviewee: I check their current job title against a list of roles that justify admin access, and I ask their manager to confirm if I'm not sure.
